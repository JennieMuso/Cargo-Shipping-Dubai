Air freight FAQs

Before you decide.

What information affects an air freight quote?

Cargo description, packed dimensions, weight, piece count, route, readiness, timing and handling requirements all matter.

Can I use estimated dimensions?

Estimates can start the discussion. Mark them clearly and provide confirmed packed measurements before the final quotation.

Is every product suitable for air freight?

No. Suitability and restrictions depend on the cargo and route. Share a precise description so the team can review it.
