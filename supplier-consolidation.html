Supplier FAQs

Before collection.

Do all suppliers need to be ready at the same time?

Share every expected date. The team can review how the timing affects collection and the wider dispatch plan.

What should each supplier provide?

Location, contact details, readiness date, cargo list, piece count, packing information, dimensions and weight where available.

Does consolidation always suit multiple orders?

No. Suitability depends on locations, timing, cargo and route. The details need review before the scope is confirmed.
