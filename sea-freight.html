Sea freight FAQs

Before you decide.

Is sea freight always the lowest-cost option?

The useful comparison is the complete shipment scope. Cargo size, route, collection, handling and delivery requirements all affect the quotation.

Do I need final measurements?

Estimates can begin the enquiry, but packed measurements and weight should be confirmed before the final scope.

Can several supplier orders be included?

Share every supplier location and readiness date so Veyra can review whether coordinated collection and consolidation are suitable.
