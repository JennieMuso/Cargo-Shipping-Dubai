What information do I need for a quote?

Tell us where the goods are in China, the UAE delivery address, what the goods are, the number of suppliers, approximate pieces and dimensions or weight, and your preferred timing.

Can goods from several suppliers be combined?

Share the supplier locations and readiness dates. We can review whether collection and consolidation are suitable for your shipment.

How do I compare air and sea freight?

Consider urgency, cargo size, handling requirements and the complete quoted scope. We can discuss suitable options once the shipment details are known.

When does a delivery window begin?

Collection, consolidation, booking, international transport, clearance and final delivery each affect timing. Any proposed window should state the point from which it is measured.

Can the final cost change?

It can if measurements, cargo details or required services change. A scoped quote should identify assumptions and conditional charges before approval.
