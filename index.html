What details do I need for a quote?

Start with your China supplier locations, UAE delivery address, cargo description, approximate pieces and size or weight, and your timing. If measurements are not ready, tell us what you know.

Can you combine goods from suppliers?

Tell us how many suppliers are involved and where they are based. We can review whether collection and consolidation can be arranged for your shipment.

Is sea or air right for my shipment?

That depends on cargo size, urgency, handling requirements and the full service scope. Share your details and we can discuss suitable options.

When does the delivery window begin?

Timing depends on collection readiness, consolidation, booking, transport, clearance and final delivery. A scoped quote should explain the assumptions behind any proposed window.
