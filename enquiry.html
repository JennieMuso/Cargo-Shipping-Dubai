What makes an enquiry useful.

You can begin with estimates. Clearly identify what is approximate and what has already been confirmed.

01

Origin details

Supplier city, address, contact, readiness date and number of collection points.

02

Cargo details

Description, pieces, packing type, dimensions, weight and handling concerns.

03

Destination details

UAE address, receiving hours, access conditions and preferred timing.
