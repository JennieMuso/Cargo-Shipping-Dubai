Getting started

Planning and quotes.

What information do I need for an enquiry?

Share the China origin, UAE destination, cargo description, supplier count, approximate pieces and dimensions or weight, plus your preferred timing.

Can I begin with approximate details?

Yes. Approximate information can start the conversation. Confirmed measurements, cargo readiness and route details are still needed before approval.

Does the website give an instant price?

No. The website helps you prepare the request. Veyra reviews the shipment and provides the customer-specific quotation directly.

Why might the final scope change?

The scope can change when measurements, cargo details, supplier readiness, route availability or required handling differ from the initial information.

Services

Routes and suppliers.

How do I compare air and sea freight?

Compare urgency, cargo size, weight, dimensions, handling needs and the complete quoted scope. The suitable option depends on the confirmed shipment.

Can goods from several suppliers be coordinated?

Share every supplier location, contact, readiness date and cargo list. Veyra can review whether coordinated collection and consolidation suit the shipment.

What affects collection planning?

Supplier location, operating hours, cargo readiness, packaging, loading access and the number of collection points all matter.

When does a delivery window begin?

Collection, consolidation, booking, international movement, clearance and final delivery each affect timing. Any proposed window should state its starting point.

Preparation

Cargo and delivery.

Why are dimensions and weight important?

They help determine space, handling and service suitability. Use packed dimensions where possible and identify which figures are estimates.

What should I share about the UAE destination?

Provide the full address, destination type, receiving hours, vehicle access limits and the expected handover arrangement.

What if my cargo is still being packed?

Share the current estimate and expected readiness date. Mark measurements as provisional so they can be confirmed before the final scope.
