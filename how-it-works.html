From details to direction.

You do not need every answer before beginning. Start with what you know, then confirm the gaps before the shipment is approved.

Prepare your details →

The shipment path.

The website helps you prepare. Veyra reviews the information, discusses the suitable scope and confirms the next action directly.

01

Share the shipment

Provide the origin, UAE destination, cargo description, supplier count and approximate size.

02

Review the gaps

The team identifies missing measurements, readiness details or handling requirements.

03

Compare the scope

Discuss the relevant route, included services, assumptions and conditional items.

04

Confirm and coordinate

After approval and capacity confirmation, collection, freight and delivery can be arranged.

Prepare these details first.

Approximate information is useful at the beginning. Confirmed measurements and readiness are still needed before the final scope.

01

Where and when

Supplier locations, readiness dates, UAE destination and preferred timing.

02

What is moving

Cargo description, number of pieces, packing type and any handling concerns.

03

How much space

Approximate dimensions, volume and weight help frame the route discussion.

04

What needs coordinating

Collection, consolidation, freight and destination access.

Air or sea?

The right comparison depends on the complete shipment. These prompts help start the conversation.

Consider sea when…

  • The stock can move on a longer planning horizon.
  • Cargo size makes sea worth comparing.
  • Supplier readiness can be coordinated around a planned dispatch.

Consider air when…

  • Timing is a priority for eligible business cargo.
  • Weight and dimensions are available for review.
  • The handling scope can be confirmed before booking.
